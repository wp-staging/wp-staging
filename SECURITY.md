## Reporting a Vulnerability

We welcome reports of security issues in WP Staging Free and WP Staging Pro.
Send them privately to support [at] wp-staging.com. Do not open a public issue.

## What a report must contain

- The affected plugin and the exact version you tested.
- A step-by-step reproduction on a default WordPress installation, with a
  working proof of concept.
- The real impact: what an attacker gains, and which role or access they need.

Reports that cannot be reproduced from what they contain are closed without
further discussion.

## Reports we close without review

- Reports generated or written by AI tools or automated scanners that were not
  verified by hand against a running installation.
- Theoretical issues without a working proof of concept.
- Issues that require full administrator access (super admin on multisite), or
  that rely on administrators being able to run code or post arbitrary JavaScript.
- Disclosure of the WP STAGING version number.
- CSV injection, missing security headers or SSL/TLS configuration, and
  content spoofing without a demonstrated attack.
- Deviations from best practices without a demonstrated impact.
- Requests for licences or builds of WP Staging Pro. We do not provide them
  for security research.

## Disclosure

Keep your findings confidential until a fix has been released and we have
agreed on disclosure with you.
